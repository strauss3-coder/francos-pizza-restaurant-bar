Our services

Eight ways to enjoy Franco's

Whether you are staying for the evening, grabbing a pizza on the way home or settling in at the bar, there is a way that suits you.

Handcrafted Pizzas

Every pizza is put together to order and baked fresh, whether it is heading to a table or into a box.

  • Made to order, never held
  • Available for dine in, takeaway or pickup
  • Featured in the Thursday and Sunday specials
Ask for the pizza menu

Restaurant Dining

Sit down, order at the table and take your time. The room is relaxed and there is no rush to move you along.

  • Table service throughout
  • Open every day from 10:00 AM
  • Full bar available at your table
Ask about a table

Takeaway & Pickup

Call the restaurant, place your order and collect it when it is ready. Straightforward and quick.

  • Order ahead by phone or WhatsApp
  • Collect in store
  • Also available on Uber Eats and Mr D
Place a takeaway enquiry

Full Bar

A properly stocked bar running from opening time to close, with something for every round.

  • Beers, wines, spirits and cocktails
  • Drink specials every day of the week
  • Draughts and bottles on tap
Ask about the bar

Cocktails

Classics shaken and poured behind the bar, from a Mojito to a Long Island Iced Tea.

  • Nine cocktails on the board
  • Shooters and mixed shots as well
  • Priced from R55
