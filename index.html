The Franco's experience

A neighbourhood table with a bar to match

Franco's brings two things together under one roof. On one side, a family restaurant serving handcrafted pizzas and hot plates. On the other, a full bar pouring beers, wines, spirits and cocktails.

Come in for lunch with the kids, meet friends for a drink after work, or collect a pizza on the way home. The pace is relaxed, the welcome is genuine and there is no dress code beyond bringing an appetite.

Handcrafted pizzas

Pizza made to order, every day

Pizza is what Franco's is known for. Every one is put together to order and baked fresh, whether you are eating in, taking it away or collecting on the way past.

The full pizza list is available in the restaurant and over the phone. Send us a message on WhatsApp and we will happily talk you through what is on offer today.
