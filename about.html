About us

A pizza restaurant with a proper bar

Franco's is a relaxed, family friendly restaurant on the corner of Ben Swart Street and 27th Avenue in Villieria, Pretoria. Handcrafted pizzas, hot plates and a full bar, all under one roof.

Home / About
Who we are

Casual dining, done properly

Franco's is the kind of place you can walk into on a Tuesday afternoon with the kids, or on a Friday night with a table of friends, and feel equally at home.

The kitchen turns out handcrafted pizzas made to order, along with the plates that fill the weekly specials board. The bar runs alongside it, pouring beers, wines, spirits and cocktails from the moment the doors open at 10:00 AM.

There is no fuss to it. Good food, cold drinks, a comfortable room and staff who are happy to see you. That is what keeps the regulars coming back.

What matters to us

Four things we get right

A family friendly atmosphere

Children are genuinely welcome here, not merely tolerated. Bring the whole family, take your time over the meal, and let the evening run at its own pace.

  • Kids eat for free on Wednesdays
  • Room for larger tables and groups

Casual dining without the fuss

No dress code, no stiff formality and no rushing you out of your seat. Franco's is somewhere you can settle in, whether that is for a quick lunch or a long evening.

  • Walk in and sit down
  • Comfortable, relaxed setting

Freshly prepared pizzas

Every pizza is put together to order and baked fresh. Eat it at the table while it is still hot, or have it boxed up to take home.

  • Made to order, never held
  • Dine in, takeaway or pickup

A relaxed restaurant and bar

The bar is part of the room rather than a separate world. Order a cocktail with your pizza, or come through just for a drink and stay for the quiz.

  • Beers, wines, spirits and cocktails
  • Quiz Night Tuesdays, Bingo Night Thursdays
